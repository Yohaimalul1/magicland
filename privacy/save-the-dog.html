Privacy Policy for Save the Dog: Draw to Rescue

Effective date: 26 October 2026 · Last updated: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Save the Dog: Draw to Rescue (Google Play package name com.IcyCornStudios.DogRescueSavetheDoggy), published on Google Play by MagicLand Apps (“we”, “us”), handles information. The app was previously published as “Dog Rescue: Save the Doggy” by an earlier developer. This policy replaces any privacy policy published for the app before, and applies to version 2.0 and later.

At a glance

  • No account or sign-in. We run no servers or analytics for this game and receive no personal data.
  • Your progress and optional nickname are saved only on your device.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google’s ads SDK collects device identifiers, IP address (approximate location), app interactions and diagnostics for advertising, ad measurement and fraud prevention.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change that choice in the game.
  • No real-money purchases. Not directed at children under 13.

Information we collect directly

We do not collect, receive or store any personal data. The game has no account or sign-in and never asks for your email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not run our own servers or analytics for this game, and the game itself does not send your game data anywhere.

Game data stored on your device

Your progress (levels reached, stars, coins, goals, win statistics, daily-reward progress) and the optional nickname you type for the Hall of Fame are saved only on your device, in the game’s local storage. This data is never sent to us.

Reset on the start screen clears your progress, coins, goals and statistics; your nickname stays until you change it (Ranks → Change name). Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of it. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include this saved data in your personal device backup, which Google handles under your Google account settings.

The Hall of Fame is an offline challenge: it compares your best level with 12 built-in champion characters. It is not an online leaderboard, and your nickname is not uploaded anywhere.

Sharing

Only if you tap Challenge or Share rank, the game opens your device’s share menu with a short text: your best level and a link to the game on Google Play. You choose which app or person receives it. We do not receive a copy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads (banners on menu and result screens, full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch) through Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. According to Google’s data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the SDK automatically collects and shares the following data with Google for advertising, analytics (ad measurement) and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general (approximate) location of your device;
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad or video views;
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage;
  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID (and, where applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device).

This data is sent directly from your device to Google and is encrypted in transit (TLS). We do not receive it in a form that identifies you. Google processes it under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, How Google uses information in advertising and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent and ad choices

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent when it starts, before ads are personalised. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use the data above in a limited way, for example for frequency capping, aggregated ad reporting and fraud prevention.

You can change your choice at any time:

  • In the game (version 2.0.1 and later): start screen → 🔒 Privacy → Privacy choices. The button appears where consent applies. The same screen has a Privacy policy button that opens this page.
  • Any version: clear the game’s storage in Android settings (Settings → Apps → Save the Dog → Storage → Clear storage). The consent form is shown again the next time the game starts. Note that this also deletes your game progress.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).

Permissions

The game requests internet and network-state access (to load ads) and the advertising ID permission used by Google’s ads SDK, plus technical permissions the ads SDK uses for background tasks. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

In-game currency

Coins are virtual, earned through gameplay, daily rewards or by choosing to watch a rewarded video, and have no real-world value. The game offers no real-money purchases.

Children’s privacy

The game is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The game asks Google to limit ads to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (maximum ad content rating “PG”). If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. On-device game data is removed by using Reset, uninstalling the game or clearing its storage. Data collected by Google for ads is retained and deleted under Google’s policies, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID as described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
